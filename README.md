
# VocalLabs AI — Product Teardown

A product management case study by **Sharmila Kummari**

---

## What is this?

VocalLabs AI is a voice AI platform that helps businesses automate phone conversations — think appointment reminders, customer support, sales calls — using AI agents that sound human.

I did a full product teardown to understand where it's working, where it's losing customers, and what to fix first.

---

## What I was trying to figure out

- Who actually gets the most value from this product?
- Why do users drop off before they see results?
- How does VocalLabs compare to its competitors?
- What should the team ship next, and in what order?

---

## How I approached it

I didn't just read about the product — I actually used it.

- Went through the full signup and onboarding flow myself
- Spoke with a VocalLabs representative to understand how they think about customers
- Benchmarked against five competitors: Retell AI, Vapi, Bland AI, Synthflow, and ElevenLabs
- Applied standard PM frameworks (ICP analysis, GTM analysis, persona mapping, activation/retention analysis)

---

## The core finding

VocalLabs' biggest problem isn't the AI — the voice quality and core tech are solid.

The real friction is everything around it: helping customers figure out what to automate, getting integrations set up, and reaching a point where the product visibly saves them time or money. Most users churn before they ever get there.

---

## Recommendations (in priority order)

**P0 — Ship these first**

1. **Outcome-Based Onboarding** — Ask users what they're trying to achieve at signup and route them to the right starting point. Right now there's no guidance at all.

2. **Healthcare Verticalization** — Healthcare customers show the highest ROI and best retention. The product should treat this as its primary market, not just one of many.

3. **Integration Readiness Center** — Connecting VocalLabs to existing CRMs, EHRs, and phone systems is the #1 deployment blocker. Pre-built connectors and a setup checklist would fix this.

**P1 — Next sprint**

4. **Homepage ICP Repositioning** — The website talks to developers. The actual buyers are operations managers and support leads. These are different people.

5. **Privacy & Trust Dashboard** — Healthcare and enterprise buyers want to see data controls upfront. Right now that information is buried or missing entirely.

## Key takeaway

VocalLabs doesn't need more AI features right now. It needs to make the features it has easier to discover, deploy, and measure. The teams that figure out time-to-value win in this space.
